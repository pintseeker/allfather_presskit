First Person, Roguelike, Dungeon Crawler

A brutal, first-person roguelike with precise, stamina-driven combat. Play as a fallen shieldmaiden who rejects the judgement of the Valkyries. Proving to the gods she deserves their favour, she uses their blessings to crawl from the depths of Helheim to the halls of Valhalla.

Features

  • Real-time first-person combat built on stamina, spacing, and timing. Perfectly timed blocks turn an enemy's attack into your opening.
  • Distinct weapons with their own movesets: sword and shield, dual axes, the longaxe, and the bow.
  • A one-way road through Helheim. Doors lock behind you, bosses seal each region, and portal choices decide which rewards you fight for.
  • Blessings from the Norse gods, offered as you level: each god pushes a different way to fight.
  • Gold to earn and spend at Brokkr's shop for relics that bend the rules of a run.
  • Permadeath with a persistent home in Hel's domain: a rune tree, weapon unlocks, and characters who remember you.
  • A dark Norse world in a deliberate PSX-era visual style.
  • An original soundtrack composed by the developer.
