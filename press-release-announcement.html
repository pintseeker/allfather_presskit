ALLFATHER Press Kit

Announcement trailer live now. Wishlist on Steam, coming 2027.

Brisbane, Australia. 29 September 2026. Solo developer Tommy today announced ALLFATHER, a brutal first-person roguelike with precise, stamina-driven combat, coming to Steam. The veteran roguelike developer follows up his hit sophomore release Gnomes with a first-person climb through Norse myth. Watch the announcement trailer here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OOHqhjWDbV0

In ALLFATHER, you play a fallen shieldmaiden who rejects the judgement of the Valkyries. Sent to Hel by mistake, she defies the will of Odin and fights her way up from the depths of Helheim to confront him in the halls of Valhalla. The other gods see an opportunity in her defiance and lend her their blessings along the way.

"Every exchange in ALLFATHER is brutal but fair. There are no cheap deaths, but you'll pay for your mistakes. It doesn't matter if you're carving through a swarm of crawling undead or in a frenetic duel with an elite. Button mashing gets you killed, awareness and patience keep you alive," said McKay.

Every run is a fresh climb through the worlds of Norse myth. Each realm is its own distinct biome: a different environmental challenge, a unique set of foes and a new boss to master. Choose your rewards at every fork, and shape your build through the blessings of the gods. Death is permanent, but the runes and iron you earn feed persistent progression in Hel's hall between runs.

"ALLFATHER is built on the roguelike foundations players know me for. Tension and real decisions. Permadeath means every fight you survive raises the stakes, and every blessing you take is a choice you live with for the rest of the run. I didn't want to make a dungeon crawl about inventory management and bookkeeping, I wanted to make one about mastering the combat system and chasing the moment where you feel your build come together."

Key Features:

  • Precise, stamina-driven first-person combat: block, parry, bash, and strike with swords, axes, bows, and shield
  • Fight outnumbered against enemies that flank and press from all sides, with readable attacks that reward awareness over button mashing
  • Earn the favour of the Norse gods: pick one of three blessings on every level up and shape a new build each run
  • Battle through the realms of the dead, each with its own enemies, environment, and boss
  • Spend hard-won gold at dwarven shops and find relics that bend the run in your favour
  • Permadeath with meta progression: unlock weapons and permanent stat upgrades in Hel's hall between runs
  • Retro PSX-style dark fantasy visuals and an original Norse soundtrack composed by Tommy himself.

About Tommy McKay

An independent developer based in Australia. A veteran roguelike developer, he is best known for the tower defense Gnomes (1750+ Overwhelmingly Positive reviews) and Sea of Survivors (400+ Very Positive reviews). Originally starting as a composer and sound designer, Tommy eventually picked up a wide skillset and became a jack of all trades. ALLFATHER is Tommy's first solo and 3D release. "It's always been a dream of mine to be able to make a game 100% solo. ALLFATHER is a chance for me to show the games industry that I'm the complete package."
